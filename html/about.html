Academic Departments of CEIT

Each department within CEIT is dedicated to providing high-quality education and innovative training in specialized technical fields.
Browse our departmental offerings to find the specific program that resonates with your passion for learning and commitment to professional growth.

Department of DIT

Focuses on the planning, installation, and management of IT infrastructure. It prepares college students to provide computing solutions and handle both hardware and software technologies.

Department of DCEEE

Covers the fields of computer engineering, electronics engineering, and electrical technology. It is often involved in industrial automation and developing innovative interconnected products.

Department of DIET

Concentrates on industrial technology, preparing students for professional careers in production, manufacturing, and technology management. It also supports national environmental and sustainability efforts.

Department of DAFE

Focuses on applying engineering principles to agro-industrial development, including soil and water resources, agricultural processing, and farm power and machinery.

Department of DCEA

Dedicated to the field of civil engineering, preparing students for careers in structural design, construction management, and urban infrastructure development.
